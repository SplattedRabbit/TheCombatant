CHARACTER 
BACKGROUND
Your character has a place in the fi  ctional world of the D&D 
game. Whether you grew up on a farm, struggled for scraps 
of bread in an alleyway, or were raised by a pack of magical 
wolves, you had a life before you started walking the road to 
adventure. The following backgrounds can serve as inspiration 
for what your character’s childhood and youth were like.
     Each background includes several headers that describe how 
you can apply it to different parts of your character’s life.
